Account and Data Deletion Policy

Time+ users have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your account using one of the following methods:

  • In-App Deletion: You can instantly delete your account and all associated data directly within the Time+ app. (If available: Navigate to Settings > Delete Account).
  • Via Email: Send an email to sahinata3506@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Time+ account. Please use the subject "Time+ Account Deletion Request".

After verifying your request, the Service Provider will process it and completely delete your account and associated personal data from our systems within 30 days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following actions are taken:

  • Your Time+ account information, including your email address, is permanently deleted from our authentication servers (Firebase).
  • All authentication-related account information is removed.
  • Account-related subscription linkages and identifiers stored by the Service Provider (RevenueCat) are permanently unlinked and anonymized.

Locally Stored Data (On Your Device)

Because Time+ prioritizes your privacy, your core productivity data (such as categories, study sessions, goals, and alarms) is stored locally on your device, not on our servers. To completely remove this data, simply uninstall the Time+ application from your device or clear the app's data in your device settings.

Data Retention Exceptions

Some strictly necessary information may be retained even after an account deletion request, solely when required by law. This includes data necessary for fraud prevention, financial and tax records (e.g., Google Play / App Store purchase receipts), dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes. Any data retained for these purposes will be strictly isolated and kept only for the minimum period required or permitted by applicable law.
