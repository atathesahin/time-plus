Time+ Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Time+ users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

Important: Deleting your Time+ account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Google Play Subscriptions.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Time+ and sign in.
  2. Open the navigation menu.
  3. Under My Account, select Delete My Account.
  4. Review the warning, confirm your identity if requested, and confirm deletion.

The Firebase Authentication account is deleted immediately. Categories, sessions, goals, alarms, settings, and other Time+ data stored locally for that account are also deleted from that device.

Request deletion by email

Email sahinata3506@gmail.com from the address associated with your Time+ account and use the subject Time+ Account Deletion Request. After identity verification, the request will be completed within 30 days.

An email request cannot remotely erase data that exists only on your device. To remove that local data, clear Time+ app data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Data deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account, email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Time+ categories, sessions, goals, alarms, and preferences stored locally on the device when deletion is performed in the app.
  • Optional Analytics collection is disabled and the Firebase Analytics installation identifier on that device is reset. Previously collected pseudonymous Analytics data is not linked to your Firebase Authentication account and follows the retention described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Other personal data controlled by the Service Provider that is not required to be retained.

Subscriptions and retained records

Subscription status and transaction data are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. Pseudonymous customer identifiers and purchase records may be retained only as needed for subscription administration, purchase restoration, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal obligations. Google Play and RevenueCat apply their own retention requirements.

For details about optional Analytics data and retention, see the Time+ Privacy Policy.
