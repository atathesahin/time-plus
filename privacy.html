Time+ Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Time+ mobile application (the “Application”), provided by Atailay Sahin (the “Service Provider”), handles information.

Privacy summary: Time+ does not contain advertising. Optional Firebase Analytics is disabled by default and is enabled only when you allow usage analytics. Core productivity data such as categories, sessions, goals, and alarms is stored locally on your device. Cloud services are used for authentication, subscription management, and optional analytics.

Information processed

  • Account information: email address, Firebase user ID, sign-in provider, and authentication metadata needed to create and secure your account.
  • Google Sign-In information: when selected, Google may provide your email address, display name, profile image, and a provider identifier to Firebase Authentication.
  • Purchase information: product, entitlement and subscription status, purchase and expiration timestamps, store transaction identifiers, and a pseudonymous customer identifier are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Technical information: Firebase and other service providers may process IP address, device or app identifiers, user-agent information, diagnostics, and security metadata necessary to operate their services.
  • Optional analytics information: if you enable usage analytics, Firebase Analytics processes app sessions, opened Time+ features, app interactions, app version, device model, operating-system information, general geographic information, and a pseudonymous Firebase installation identifier. Time+ does not send email addresses, Firebase user IDs, notes, category names, alarm titles, or other productivity content to Analytics.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting the Service Provider.

Locally stored Time+ data

Categories, sessions, notes, tags, timers, goals, alarms, theme and language preferences are stored on your device rather than on a Time+ server. This data does not automatically synchronize to another device. Export and import occur only when you initiate them and choose a file location.

How information is used

  • To create, authenticate, secure, and delete Time+ accounts.
  • To provide, restore, and validate Premium subscriptions and enforce free-plan limits.
  • To provide alarms, timers, local notifications, text-to-speech, export/import, and requested support.
  • If you opt in, to understand aggregated feature usage, evaluate the testing experience, and improve Time+.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate failures, comply with law, and protect users and the Service Provider.

Personal information is not sold. Time+ does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

Permissions and device features

Time+ may request notification, exact alarm, full-screen alarm, vibration, wake-lock, boot-completed, and foreground-service capabilities so user-created timers and alarms can operate as intended. Text-to-speech is provided by the speech engine installed on your device. Permission use is limited to the related feature.

Service providers

  • Google Play Services and Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication and optional Firebase Analytics
  • RevenueCat

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and may process data in countries other than yours using safeguards required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

  • Local Time+ data remains on the device until you delete it in the app, clear app data, replace it during import, or uninstall the app.
  • Firebase account information remains until the account is deleted, subject to limited security or legal retention.
  • Purchase and subscription records may be retained by Google Play, RevenueCat, or the Service Provider as required for subscription administration, restoration, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, or law.
  • Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.
  • Optional Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of the Time+ Google Analytics property. Disabling Analytics stops future collection on that device.

Delete your account in Time+ under My Account → Delete My Account, or follow the external Account and Data Deletion instructions. Deleting a Time+ account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, including encrypted network connections provided by Firebase, Google Play, and RevenueCat. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

Time+ is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required in their country. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Contact the Service Provider to request removal if you believe a child provided personal information.

Your choices and rights

Usage analytics is optional and disabled by default. You can allow or disable it at any time from the Time+ menu under Optional usage analytics. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. Contact the Service Provider to exercise an applicable right.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Time+ features, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised date will be shown at the top of this page.
